    CRBN Rig

    Play your guitar through captures of real gear. CRBN Rig is by GOATi Entertainment, part of the CRBN suite, and is also a desktop app on Pavillion Hub with lower latency through your audio interface.comes from Pavillion Hub; its web version is at rig.carbonlinear.com.

    Based on Neural Amp Modeler by Steven Atkinson. It plays NAM captures, such as those shared on TONE3000; captures and IRs belong to their authors and are not part of CRBN Rig. The demo riff and example capture come from Neural Amp Modeler Core.
