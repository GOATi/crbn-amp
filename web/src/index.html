    CRBN Rig

    Play your guitar through captures of real gear. CRBN Rig is by GOATi Entertainment, part of the CRBN suite, and is also a desktop app on Pavillion Hub with lower latency through your audio interface.

    Based on Neural Amp Modeler by Steven Atkinson. It plays NAM captures, such as those shared on TONE3000; captures and IRs belong to their authors and are not part of CRBN Rig. The demo riff and example capture come from Neural Amp Modeler Core.

    Your files stay in your browser: nothing you load is uploaded.
