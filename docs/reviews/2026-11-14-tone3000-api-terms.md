# TONE3000's API and its terms, for build plan R8 (U7)

**2026-11-14, SYNC:RIG-030 addendum.** R8 says: read TONE3000's API and its terms for API clients first, and bring them
to the owner with a recommendation. This reads public pages only (no account, no key, no call to the API). An agent
gathered it; the session checked the API terms' key wording against the live page (https://www.tone3000.com/api/terms)
itself. Quotes should be checked against the live pages again before anything is built: the terms are new (effective
2026-07-07) and may move.

## What there is

- **A public REST API, v1** (https://www.tone3000.com/api; launched in beta June 2025,
  https://www.tone3000.com/blog/introducing-the-tone3000-api). Keys are **self-serve** in account settings: a publishable
  key (the OAuth `client_id`) and a server-only secret, with redirect URIs registered there. "Evaluating and developing
  against the API is free."
- **Sign-in:** OAuth 2.0 with PKCE for every integration, refresh tokens, and an RFC 8628 device flow (approved at
  tone3000.com/activate). The web version can use the redirect flow; the desktop app a loopback redirect or the device
  flow. Neither needs to hold a secret.
- **Prompt flows:** `select_tone` opens TONE3000's own picker (with audition players) and returns a tone id; `load_tone`
  checks the person can still open a tone id the app kept.
- **Endpoints:** the person (`/user`); lists (`/tones/created`, `/favorited`, `/downloaded`, `/trending`, `/latest`);
  search (`/tones/search`, filters by gear, make, tag, creator, format, architecture; "heavily rate-limited by
  default"); favourites (`PUT`/`DELETE /tones/{id}/favorite`); captures (`/models?tone_id=`, `/models/{id}`, each with a
  `model_url` that expires after about an hour); `/models/lookup?content_hash=` (finds TONE3000's entry for a local file
  by NAM's content hash); `/makes`, `/tags`. A whole tone as a zip is for approved partners only.
- **Each tone carries its creator's licence:** `t3k`, CC BY, BY-SA, BY-NC, BY-NC-SA, BY-ND, BY-NC-ND, or CC0. T3K lets a
  person use the file in software and publish what they make with it, but not "upload, republish, or distribute the data
  file without the author's permission". Rig must never pass a capture from one person to another.
- **Example code:** https://github.com/tone-3000/t3k-api (MIT): Select, Load Tone and a "Full API" custom browser, in
  web, Electron, JUCE and mobile frameworks, with a dependency-free `tone3000-client.ts`. No sandbox was found.
- **Precedent:** TONE3000's own plug-in (on the same API) and hardware such as HeadRush, Darkglass Anagram, Sonulab
  StompStation, Blackstar Beam and PiPedal integrate it.

## The terms that decide what Rig may build (https://www.tone3000.com/api/terms)

- **Two tiers.** Free, for non-commercial products, with no sign-off ("access may be revoked"). Commercial: "If you
  charge for your product, or your product promotes or accompanies a paid product, a commercial agreement is
  required"; commercial products get the full API and "must be reviewed and signed off by TONE3000 before they are
  publicly published or announced".
- **What the free tier may use: unclear.** The live page, read by the session: "Free tier integrations may only use the
  OAuth prompt flows (select_tone and load_tone)". The agent's reading also listed the bounded lists (favourited,
  downloaded, created, trending, latest) as free. Either way, search, favouriting and the captures endpoint (which
  holds the file's `model_url`) look like the full API.
- **Branding:** "Powered by TONE3000" with a link; browsing, search and delivery presented "as TONE3000, under TONE3000
  branding"; each tone in a list shows its image, gear, format and creator; a persistent way into the full catalogue
  through the Select flow. Creator names, metadata and licences are never stripped or changed.
- **Downloads:** "Do not bulk download, scrape, mirror, or cache the TONE3000 catalog." "Only download models when a
  user requests them." Content per user, authenticated; a token only for the person who granted it. Nothing found
  about keeping a capture the person chose for offline play.
- **Rate limits:** 100 requests a minute by default, search lower; "Respect rate limits and do not circumvent them."
- **Not allowed:** a competing tone platform or catalogue, reselling access, white-labelling. The general Terms of
  Service (https://www.tone3000.com/terms) forbid automated scraping and collecting "all or a substantial portion" of
  the tones.

## What this means for U7 as planned

P3-UX's U7 ("browse, favourite and play TONE3000's captures in Rig's own library") is a custom browser: search,
favourites and downloads through the API, drawn in Rig's library. Under these terms that is the **full API**, which
reads as the commercial tier, sign-off before release, and TONE3000's branding on that part of the library. Whether
Rig is "commercial" is itself open: it is free, but it ships through Pavillion Hub beside other suite apps, and the
terms count a product that "accompanies a paid product".

## Recommendation, for the owner's word

1. **Build it only through the official OAuth API**, never by reading the site.
2. **Start with what is clearly allowed:** "Browse TONE3000" opens their Select flow (PKCE on the web, the device or
   loopback flow on the desktop), and the capture chosen plays in Rig and joins the library with its creator, licence
   and "Powered by TONE3000" shown, kept only on that person's computer.
3. **Before the browser inside Rig's library, write to TONE3000** (support@tone3000.com): what the free tier covers
   (the lists, `/models` and its download); whether Rig in Pavillion Hub counts as commercial; whether a chosen
   capture may be kept for offline play; their design guidelines and a production rate limit; and their sign-off on
   the integration before it is announced. Writing to them is the owner's to do, or to ask a session to draft.
