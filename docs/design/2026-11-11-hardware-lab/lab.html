Knobs, switches, lights and faceplates

Twenty hardware kits for CRBN Rig. Each draws the amp and its pedals as one family, with the real controls: Input, Gate, Bass, Middle, Treble and Output, the Gate and EQ switches, and the pilot light with Power and Standby. Ten are classic, after the gear players already know. Nine are new, with the energy from Pavillion Hub's logo window inside the amp, behind cut faceplates and the meshes between. The twentieth is yours, CRBN, built in the builder and now the default. Turn the knobs, flip the switches, stomp the pedals: everything on this page is live.

How to pick
  • Keep, Maybe or Cut on each kit: the ones you keep become players' choices in the look menu.
  • Make it the default on the one Rig opens in. Try it on the page puts a kit on Rig's page above.
  • A note on any kit is saved as you type. The questions at the end settle how the kits behave.
  • Switch the finish, the look and Playing in the bar: a recorded riff drives the lights and the energy (no sound).

Classic kits are after the gear named, never copies of it: no maker's name, logo, script or panel layout ships, and every amp's name is Rig's or the loaded capture's. Today's page is at rig.carbonlinear.com.

Version 5: your notes on Salvage
  • Under Salvage are the choices players will get: the sheet, the grille, the grille's metal and the glow behind it, with Shuffle for new crops.
  • Six grilles: expanded metal, chicken wire, woven wire, perforated sheet, welded grid and bar grate.
  • Grilles in old bare metal, with no paint: old steel, galvanised, rusted iron and aged copper. The same scrap is still there too.
  • Five glows: embers, valves, furnace, neon, or none.
  • Every word is stamped into the metal, with a shadow on its upper wall and light on its lower lip. On light metal the stamp reads dark. On dark metal it shows bright bare steel, where the punch broke the paint. That includes CRBN RIG and SALVAGE on the plate and the pedals' names. The capture's label-maker tape keeps its raised letters.
