Knobs, switches, lights and faceplates

Nineteen hardware kits for CRBN Rig: each draws the amp and its pedals as one family, with the real controls (Input, Gate, Bass, Middle, Treble and Output, the Gate and EQ switches, the pilot light with Power and Standby). Ten are classic, after the gear players already know. Nine are new: the energy from Pavillion Hub's logo window sits inside the amp, behind cut faceplates and the meshes between. Turn the knobs, flip the switches, stomp the pedals. Everything on this page is live.

How to pick
  • Keep, Maybe or Cut on each kit: the ones you keep become players' choices in the look menu.
  • Make it the default on the one Rig opens in. Try it on the page puts a kit on Rig's page above.
  • A note on any kit is saved as you type. The questions at the end settle how the kits behave.
  • Switch the finish, the look and Playing in the bar: a recorded riff drives the lights and the energy (no sound).

Classic kits are after the gear named, never copies of it: no maker's name, logo, script or panel layout ships, and every amp's name is Rig's or the loaded capture's. Today's page is at rig.carbonlinear.com.

Energy inside the amp. Each kit puts one of the Hub's logo-window effects (or a new one) behind a faceplate with cutouts, and a mesh between. The energy answers your playing: level, pick attack and the Input knob. A slot of light round every knob shows its setting.
